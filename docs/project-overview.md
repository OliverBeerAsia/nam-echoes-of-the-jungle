# Project Overview

## Premise

At the tail end of the Vietnam War, the player (Sgt. Miller) survives a helicopter crash in rural southern Vietnam. The squad is scattered. The primary objective is to find surviving colleagues, regroup, and reach the nearest ARVN base.

## Experience Goals

- RPG first, shooter second.
- Story pressure comes from people and choices, not just body count.
- Combat is dangerous and costly, and should often feel avoidable.
- Civilian trust and mission choices affect outcomes.

## Core Player Loop

1. Explore an area and gather leads through conversation, documents, and landmarks.
2. Complete quest steps that unlock new routes, allies, or intel.
3. Decide how to handle conflict: stealth, negotiation, diversion, or force.
4. Regroup with survivors and push toward safer ground (ARVN lines).

## Current Vertical Slice

- Three squadmate outcomes tracked: `rodriguez`, `cpl_whitaker`, `spc_hale`.
- Supporting civilian arcs: Thanh and Duc, village trust chain, convoy protection.
- Route progression: Crash Site -> Village -> VC Camp -> Clinic -> River Crossing -> Hamlet -> ARVN Outpost.
- Final gate is blocked until checkpoint protocol is resolved and required companions are regrouped.

## Gameplay Reference Targets

- Tactical pace: deliberate movement, risk management, limited resources.
- Quest density: frequent short goals that build into long-form mission arcs.
- Dialogue impact: choices should open or close later options.
- Moral accounting: "Hearts and Minds" should matter as much as kills.

## Success Criteria

- Players can complete meaningful progress without constant firefights.
- At least one major quest branch favors nonviolent or low-violence play.
- Endings clearly reflect who was saved, who was abandoned, and how civilians were treated.
