# Narrative Beats

## Beat 1: Crash And Orientation

- Miller regains control near burning wreckage.
- Player secures map fragment and reaches the village.
- Tone: disorientation, urgency, survival.

## Beat 2: Trust Or Threat

- Village NPCs provide fragmented intel on missing squadmates.
- Player can gain cooperation through service or coercion.
- Trust trajectory starts here and propagates through all later outcomes.

## Beat 3: Rodriguez Rescue

- VC camp introduces highest immediate combat pressure.
- Optional radio sabotage delays response but increases escalation risk.
- Rodriguez serves as bridge to Whitaker/Hale leads.

## Beat 4: Whitaker Stabilization

- Mission clinic confirms Whitaker's condition.
- Field kit can be obtained cooperatively or by force.
- Stabilization transitions mission from rescue to escort/regroup.

## Beat 5: Crossing Decision

- Ferry route can be negotiated or seized.
- Decision directly affects trust and pace into eastern sectors.
- Crossing completion unlocks Hale arc.

## Beat 6: Hale's Moral Line

- Hale is blocked by civilian risk, not fear alone.
- Player can protect convoy (trust-positive) or force compliance (trust-negative).
- Rejoining Hale enables full regroup state.

## Beat 7: ARVN Perimeter Push

- Squad reaches outpost perimeter under tension.
- Gate remains blocked until regroup and checkpoint protocol are satisfied.
- ARVN officers provide final narrative framing of player conduct.

## Beat 8: Outcome Framing

- Ending tier resolves from companions regrouped + civilian trust + conflict style.
- Best result reflects restraint and collective survival.
- Failure emphasizes strategic success but moral/relational loss.
