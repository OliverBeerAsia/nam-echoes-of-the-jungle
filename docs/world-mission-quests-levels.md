# World Mission Quests Levels

## Setting

- Time: tail end of the Vietnam War.
- Place: rural southern Vietnam across jungle approaches, village zones, and ARVN perimeter routes.
- Context: Sgt. Miller survives a helicopter crash and must regroup the squad while managing local trust.

## Mission Statement

Find surviving squadmates in different circumstances, regroup, and reach the nearest ARVN base with as many people alive as possible.

## Campaign Arc

1. `Aftershock`: crash survival and navigation recovery.
2. `Hearts of the Village`: trust-building for routes and intel.
3. `Rescue`: recover Rodriguez from VC custody.
4. `Medic Down` + `Field Surgery`: locate and stabilize Whitaker.
5. `Silent Crossing`: secure river route.
6. `Radio Ghost`: find and recruit Hale.
7. `Convoy to ARVN`: move regrouped squad toward ARVN lines.
8. `Last Checkpoint`: clear final gate protocol and enter outpost.

## Level Flow

- Crash Site: early survival and map recovery.
- Village: primary dialogue and trust hub.
- VC Camp: rescue/sabotage pressure point.
- Mission Clinic: Whitaker recovery branch.
- River Crossing: negotiated or forced route control.
- River Hamlet: Hale trust/morale branch.
- ARVN Outpost: checkpoint resolution and finale trigger.

## Narrative Roster

- Squadmates: Rodriguez, Cpl. Whitaker, Spc. Hale.
- Local civilians/allies: Elder Nguyen, Thanh, Duc, Father Bao, Sister Lan, Binh, Ferryman Huy, Mai.
- ARVN personnel: Lt. Pham, Sgt. Kiet.

## Ending Matrix

- `best`: all required squadmates regrouped, high civilian trust, sufficient nonviolent resolutions.
- `good`: most squadmates regrouped with stable trust.
- `fail`: ARVN reached with poor regroup/trust outcomes.
