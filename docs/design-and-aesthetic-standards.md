# Design And Aesthetic Standards

## Tone Standards

- Serious and grounded, never arcade-comedic.
- Human-centered war story, not hero power fantasy.
- Tension should come from uncertainty, exhaustion, and moral tradeoffs.

## Visual Direction

- Jungle military palette: olive, mud, amber, smoke, worn metal, faded cloth.
- UI should feel field-issued: readable, minimal, utilitarian.
- Keep screen clutter low so world readability supports exploration.
- Avoid flashy effects that undermine grounded tone.

## UI Standards

- Objectives are clear, short, and location-oriented.
- Dialogue choices are concise and consequences are legible.
- Notifications should support decision making, not noise.
- Inventory and journal should reinforce role-play and context.

## Audio Standards

- Ambient jungle audio drives mood more than music.
- Weapon and explosion audio should signal danger and cost.
- Quiet moments after combat are important for pacing.

## Narrative Standards

- Respectful portrayal of local civilians and ARVN forces.
- Avoid framing all NPCs as hostile.
- Side quests should reveal people, not just loot.
- Squadmate arcs should differ in circumstance and emotional tone.

## Violence Budget

- Combat should be meaningful, not constant.
- Encounters should support stealth, bypass, or de-escalation where possible.
- "Best" outcomes should not require maximum enemy kills.
